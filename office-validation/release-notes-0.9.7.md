Office Ambilight now has a BetterTrumpet-style lighting mixer: click the tray
icon once for master and per-device intensity; double-click for the full app.
Each device has its own mute/restore control, and offline devices retain their
levels. Pinning and collapse keep the controller convenient while watching.

Fullscreen intensity is a shared percentage in the main app's Output card,
defaulting to50%. It dims lighting for fullscreen apps on the main display and
restores normal levels afterward. Master and device defaults remain100%.

The flyout and refreshed context menu use a matching green-teal palette, rounded
surfaces and clearer hover states. Existing pause, screen and Awake options remain.

Master mute releases image capture; gamma, Awake, rest/off and calibration policy
remain independent. Slider persistence, output scaling, reconnect, stale edits
and app-owned UI rendering passed hosted Windows checks. Delivery preserves
existing settings and the prior0.9.6 rollback installation.
